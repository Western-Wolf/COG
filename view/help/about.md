Custom Overlay Gadgets (COG)

COG is a Prisma UI HUD toolkit for Skyrim Special Edition. It has built-in trackers for most vanilla statistics: everything from an oxygen meter to a werewolf timer.
Individual trackers are known as Gears. Gears are exposed in Gadgets.

COG Gadgets are split into Groups, Gauges, and Gizmos. See Definitions for details.

Mods can register their own named trackers (Gears), or their own bespoke widgets (Gizmos) using the Papyrus `COG_API`.

Open and close the editor with the bound key (default **F3**). Click that key label in the top-right to rebind it.

Changes save live under `SKSE/Plugins/COG/`.
